Harper is a graduate student researching speech processing at Johns Hopkins University, advised by Dr. Berrak Sisman. Working with language audio data brings together Harper's experience as an engineer, their technical and analytical interests, and expertise transforming sound.

Harper is eager to learn from varied language experiences, such as users of Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC), multilingual individuals, and speakers of languages with fewer digital resources. They look forward to ongoing exploration of linguistics, accessibility, and building robust speech synthesis tools.
